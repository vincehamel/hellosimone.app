AI assistant

AI does the work. You keep the decisions.

Simone's assistant takes on the repetitive work. Each task has a set level of autonomy, based on its risk.

  • I handle it

    Reminders, checks, reports and alerts: no money out, nothing irreversible.

  • I prepare, you approve

    Anything involving money, a client or an official document: drafted, then approved in one click.

  • I help you

    Variances to explain, forecasts, board packs: the assistant prepares, you decide.

What an agent will never do

Pay or transfer money. Contact a client without your approval. Change an issued invoice, bank details or a closed month. See more than you do.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to switch accounting software?

No. Simone can run your full accounting or work alongside your current software. We choose together based on your situation.

Who is it for?

Service SMBs that sell time or projects: agencies, studios, professional services firms, consultants. From one person to a team of about a hundred.

Is my data protected?

Each company has its own isolated environment. Two-factor sign-in, bank details encrypted and always masked, and every action logged.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Hours, expenses and approvals work on a phone as well as on a computer, with no app to install.

How much does Simone cost?

Pricing depends on team size and the modules you turn on. It is presented in the demo, along with setup.
